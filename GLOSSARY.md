# Sports Schedule

A personal schedule of events from followed leagues, narrowed by favourite teams and time.

## Language

**Followed league**:
A league a person has saved to their schedule.

**Favourite team**:
A team a person has saved independently of their followed leagues.
_Avoid_: Selected team, which refers to an active filter rather than a saved preference.

**Schedule selection**:
The current combination of league, team, Favourites/All, and schedule period used to narrow the schedule.

**Schedule period**:
One of Upcoming, Live, Today, or Tomorrow, interpreted in the viewer's local time.

**Estimated playing window**:
The interval from an event's scheduled start through its sport's estimated duration.

**Live score**:
The latest available home and away scores and reported status for an event within its estimated playing window.
